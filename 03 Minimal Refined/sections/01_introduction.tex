\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
An effective preprint should make its central scientific question clear within
its first page. Introduce the setting, identify the limitation of existing
methods, and state the evidence needed to evaluate the claim. A recent research
literature example can be cited using \citet{vaswani2017attention}.

\paragraph{Contributions.} Summarize the main contributions precisely:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A well-defined problem and transparent evaluation protocol.
  \item An independently reproducible method with controlled comparisons.
  \item Empirical findings with appropriate uncertainty and limitations.
\end{itemize}

In a real manuscript, continue with a motivating figure or a concise overview
of your results rather than using decorative material.
